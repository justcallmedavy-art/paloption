Responsible Trading

Trade Responsibly

At StrategyOption, we care about your well-being. Trading should be enjoyable and within your means. Here are some guidelines to help you trade responsibly.

Set a Budget

Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Never use funds needed for essential expenses.

Take Breaks

Set time limits for your trading sessions. Regular breaks help maintain focus and clear thinking.

Know the Risks

Understand that trading involves risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Emotional Control

Don't trade when upset, tired, or under the influence. Emotional trading leads to poor decisions.

Trading Guidelines

Do
  • Set daily and weekly trading limits
  • Keep records of your trades
  • Learn and understand the markets
  • Use demo accounts to practice
  • Take regular breaks from trading
  • Seek help if trading becomes a problem
Don't
  • Chase losses with bigger trades
  • Trade with borrowed money
  • Let trading affect your relationships
  • Trade under emotional distress
  • Ignore warning signs of addiction
  • Risk more than you can afford

Warning Signs

If you recognize any of these behaviors, consider taking a break or seeking support:

Trading more than you planned
Feeling anxious when not trading
Hiding trading activity from others
Neglecting work or relationships
Borrowing money to trade
Unable to stop despite losses
